Transfer Target Optimisation Model

A budget-constrained optimisation model for transfer window decisions, built by directing AI tools.

Deciding who to sign in a transfer window usually comes down to a scouting judgement call. I wanted to reframe it as an optimisation problem instead: given financial constraints and a pool of scouted targets, which combination of signings gets the most value without breaking the budget?

I built this because I'm genuinely interested in the finance side of football. I don't code myself, but I've learned to direct AI tools like Claude to turn my own logic and assumptions into a working model on Excel.

The model scores targets on utility, weighs them against cost and positional need, and solves for the best combination given the budget. It's the same logic that sits behind real transfer committee decisions, just made explicit and testable instead of left to gut feel.

Football's transfer market still runs largely on scouting relationships and valuation by feel, with financial discipline applied loosely at best. This is a small attempt at bringing some of that decision-making into a structured, repeatable framework, the sort of thing that could genuinely sit inside a recruitment or finance department rather than staying an academic exercise.
